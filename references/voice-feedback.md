# Voice feedback without a house formula

Use an approved owner-specific packet, not a generic ideal of “human writing.” Store a small set of paired passages with exact owner response, source/version, intended format, and public-use boundary. Separate accepted wording, rejected wording, and the editor's proposed explanation. An acceptance applies to that piece; it does not establish a universal rule.

Review stance, degree of certainty, what the owner chooses to explain, relationship between observation and conclusion, cadence, and vocabulary. A smooth sentence can still replace the author's judgment. Prefer the better-supported version, not automatically the shorter one. Ask about a material voice ambiguity rather than manufacturing quirks.
