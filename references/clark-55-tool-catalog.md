# Paraphrased 55-tool operational catalog

This catalog uses original working labels and paraphrases rather than reproducing Clark's chapter text. Tool numbers preserve the verified edition order.

Review reach:

- `Direct`: normally available during draft review without changing the story contract.
- `Structural`: may require paragraph, scene, or narrative reorganization; reopen story lock if meaning changes.
- `Workflow`: primarily governs research, drafting, collaboration, or revision practice rather than sentence repair.

## Tools 1–10 — sentence mechanics and pruning

| # | Working interpretation | Diagnose when | Expected impact | Guardrail | Reach |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Put the actor and action early | Readers wait too long to learn who did what | Faster initial comprehension | A delayed opening may be intentional for suspense, transition, or rhythm | Direct |
| 2 | Reserve sentence edges for emphasis | The key idea is buried mid-sentence | Better salience and recall | Do not force every sentence into a dramatic landing | Direct |
| 3 | Replace weak constructions with active verbs | Nominalizations, helper verbs, or vague action drain energy | More movement, fewer words, clearer agency | Preserve technical precision and owner cadence | Direct |
| 4 | Use passive focus deliberately | The affected person or result matters more than the actor, or the actor is unknown | Correct emphasis and ethical focus | Passive voice must not conceal responsible actors | Direct |
| 5 | Make every adverb earn its place | An adverb repeats the verb or supplies vague intensity | Greater precision and trust | Keep adverbs that materially change meaning or belong to the owner's voice | Direct |
| 6 | Reduce unnecessary continuous forms | Chains of `-ing` forms obscure time or weaken action | Cleaner tense and chronology | Continuous action may be the exact meaning | Direct |
| 7 | Use controlled long sentences | Related ideas have been chopped into exhausting fragments | Sustained logical or narrative flow | The reader must still retain the subject, direction, and hierarchy | Direct |
| 8 | Build a pattern and then break it | Parallel units feel predictable or the payoff lacks surprise | Rhythm followed by emphasis or reversal | Avoid formulaic three-beat LinkedIn rhetoric | Direct |
| 9 | Let punctuation shape movement | Meaning is clear but breath, grouping, or pace is wrong | Better cadence and relationship signaling | Punctuation cannot rescue overloaded thinking | Direct |
| 10 | Remove structural excess before word-level excess | The draft is bloated at section and sentence levels | Larger clarity gain with less fussy polishing | Do not cut owned scenes or evidence merely to shorten | Direct |

| # | Working interpretation | Diagnose when | Expected impact | Guardrail | Reach |

| 34 | Change narrative distance and angle | A scene is spatially flat or hard to picture | Cinematic orientation and emphasis | Do not fabricate physical detail or over-direct a short post | Structural |
| 35 | Build with scenes and sequence them | The draft summarizes lived experience without rendering decisive moments | Experience, proof, and narrative movement | A scene must be sourced and earn its space | Structural |
| 36 | Combine narrative and explanatory modes | Pure story lacks interpretation or pure exposition lacks experience | Richer transfer of fact and meaning | Mode changes need clear transitions and purpose | Structural |

| 39 | Revise in the direction of the ending | The ending feels appended or earlier material does not support it | Closure and cumulative meaning | Do not reverse-engineer false inevitability | Structural |

## Operational conclusion

The 55 tools are not equally available at the same moment:

- Direct draft review should retrieve from a relevant subset, usually zero to three tools per finding.
- Structural tools require story-contract protection and may send the draft backward in the workflow.
- Workflow tools should configure how work is done; they should not be mechanically applied to finished sentences.
- A valid review may apply no Clark tool when the diagnosed passage already performs its job.
